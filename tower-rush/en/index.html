FAQ

A purchase did not arrive

Tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the shop. If it still does not arrive, email us with the purchase date and the order ID from Apple's receipt.

Moving to a new device

Save data is stored only on your device. Create a transfer code in "Profile" on the old device and load it on the new one. Paid gems cannot be restored with "Restore purchases" because they are consumable.

Stop ad tracking

Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device. Every feature works without allowing tracking.

Delete my data

Use Settings > "Delete save data" in the app, or delete the app. All data on the device is erased.

Cancel the subscription

Go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your device. You keep access until the end of the current period.

Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026 / Revised September 24, 2026

Data that stays on your device

The App stores save data such as your player name, progress, and items on your device. This data is not sent to the Company's servers.

The transfer code is created and loaded by you. The Company does not keep it.

Data the Company collects

Currently, the App does not automatically send any data to the Company.

If you contact us, we keep your email address and message only as needed to reply.

Data collected by third parties

In-app purchases are processed by Apple Inc. and are subject to Apple's privacy policy. The Company never receives your card number.

To verify purchases and deliver what you bought, the App uses RevenueCat, Inc. Your purchase history and an anonymous identifier generated by the App are sent to RevenueCat. Your name and email address are not sent.

The App shows ads through AdMob by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob collects your IP address (used to estimate your general location), device identifiers, ad interactions such as views and taps, and app performance and crash information.

AdMob collects the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it in the "Allow App to Track" prompt shown the first time. You can use every feature without allowing it; in that case ads are not based on your interests. You can change this at any time in your device Settings.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent to ads through Google's consent management tool.

RevenueCat and Google may process information outside Japan, including in the United States. Each company handles it under its own privacy policy.

Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ / RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy / Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Access, correction, deletion

Save data is on your device and is fully erased when the App is deleted.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information from a support inquiry, contact us below.

For information collected by RevenueCat or Google, you can also contact those companies directly.

Email: guerrilla.dcg@gmail.com
